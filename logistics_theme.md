# Logistics Site Orange Theme Style Guide

## Color Palette
- **Primary Background**: #FFFFFF (Pure White)
- **Secondary Background**: #FFF8F3 (Very Light Orange)
- **Accent Background**: #FEF7F0 (Soft Orange Tint)
- **Primary Orange**: #FF6B35 (Vibrant Orange)
- **Secondary Orange**: #FF8C42 (Medium Orange)
- **Light Orange**: #FFB366 (Soft Orange)
- **Dark Orange**: #E55A2B (Deep Orange)
- **Text Primary**: #2D3748 (Dark Gray)
- **Text Secondary**: #4A5568 (Medium Gray)
- **Text Light**: #718096 (Light Gray)
- **Success Green**: #38A169 (Green for positive elements)
- **Warning Yellow**: #D69E2E (Yellow for highlights)
- **Border Color**: #E2E8F0 (Light Border)

## Typography
- **Primary Font**: 'Open Sans', sans-serif (Clean, readable)
- **Heading Font**: 'Pathway Gothic One', sans-serif (Bold, distinctive)
- **Font Weights**: 400 (regular), 600 (semi-bold), 700 (bold)

## Design Elements
- **Button Primary**: #FF6B35 background, #FFFFFF text
- **Button Secondary**: #FFFFFF background, #FF6B35 border and text
- **Button Hover**: #E55A2B (darker orange)
- **Link Color**: #FF6B35 (primary orange)
- **Link Hover**: #E55A2B (dark orange)
- **Card Shadow**: Subtle gray shadows
- **Border Radius**: 8px for modern look

## Component Styles
- **Navigation**: White background with orange accents
- **Hero Section**: Clean white background with orange highlights
- **Cards**: White background with orange borders on hover
- **Buttons**: Rounded corners with solid orange colors
- **Icons**: Orange color scheme throughout

## Orange Theme Features
- **Warm and Trustworthy**: Orange conveys reliability and approachability
- **High Visibility**: Orange stands out and draws attention to key elements
- **Professional Logistics**: Orange is associated with energy and movement
- **Modern Design**: Clean lines with orange accents for contemporary feel

This orange theme creates a warm, trustworthy, and professional appearance perfect for a logistics company that wants to appear reliable, energetic, and customer-focused.
